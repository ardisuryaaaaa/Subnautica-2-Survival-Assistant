## 🐚 Overview

Beneath the waves of **Subnautica 2**, every diver learns the same lesson: the ocean does not negotiate. Pressure crushes, predators stalk, and oxygen is a currency you spend with every heartbeat. **Subnautica-2-God-Mode-Trainer-Deluxe** is a companion utility that reshapes that harsh economy into something gentler — a personal sanctuary beneath the surface where curiosity outruns fear. Whether you are charting biome edges, cataloging leviathan behavior, or simply building a habitat that defies gravity and depth, this trainer gives you the reins.

This is not a shortcut past the experience; it is a lens that reframes it. The ocean remains vast, mysterious, and beautifully hostile — you simply stop being its casualty.

## ✨ Feature Arsenal

### 🛡️ Invincibility & Damage Nullification
Activate a persistent shield layer that absorbs predator strikes, thermal vents, and crushing pressure alike. Damage events are intercepted before they reach your health pool, letting you observe the world without flinching.

### 💨 Infinite Oxygen
Your tanks never empty. Explore the deepest trenches, wander through lava zones, and linger in kelp forests without the rhythmic panic of surfacing. The breath bar becomes a decoration rather than a countdown.

### 🚀 Fast Craft Propulsion
Submersibles, seaglides, and personal propulsion units gain a configurable velocity multiplier. Cross biomes in seconds, or dial it back for cinematic sweeps through coral archways.

### 🎚️ Speed Control Module
A granular slider governs your movement — from a thoughtful drift to a blur that would make a ghost leviathan jealous. Speed is a dial, not a switch.

### 🧭 Threat Awareness Toggle
Predators still exist, but their aggression can be softened. Track their patrol routes from a safe distance and learn the rhythm of the food chain without becoming part of it.

### 🏗️ Habitat Persistence Boost
Structures resist environmental wear, letting you build elaborate outposts in volatile regions without constant maintenance.

### 🌐 Responsive UI Overlay
A lightweight in-game panel scales cleanly across resolutions, from ultrawide monitors to compact laptops. No clutter — just the controls you need, where you expect them.

### 🗣️ Multilingual Support
Interface strings ship in multiple languages, with community-contributed translations expanding regularly. The ocean speaks every tongue.

### 🕛 24/7 Customer Support
A rotating support desk answers questions around the clock — because the ocean never sleeps, and neither does the help channel.

### 📴 Offline-First Architecture
Every function runs locally. No telemetry, no mandatory online handshake, no phantom connections. Your play session remains yours alone.

### 🔄 Snapshot & Restore
Save your current trainer configuration as a profile and reload it instantly. Switch between "explorer mode" and "cinematic mode" in one click.

## 🛠️ Configuration Guide

The trainer reads a plain-text configuration file placed alongside the executable. Adjust values there or through the in-game overlay. All parameters are hot-reloadable — no restart required.

Key settings include:
- `shield_mode` — enable or disable damage interception
- `oxygen_lock` — hold breath meter at maximum
- `craft_multiplier` — velocity scaling factor
- `overlay_scale` — UI size adjustment
- `language` — interface locale
- `profile_slot` — active saved profile

## 🔐 Privacy & Security Posture

This utility operates entirely on your local machine. It does not phone home, does not collect analytics, and does not require an account. Configuration files stay on disk. There are no background network sockets, no updater daemons, and no hidden processes. What you see is what runs.

## 📖 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this modify game files permanently?**
No. All changes are applied at runtime and vanish when the session ends.

**Will this affect my save games?**
Save data remains untouched. The trainer operates in memory only.

**Can I use this alongside other utilities?**
Yes, though overlapping functionality may cause conflicts. Disable redundant modules for best results.

**Is there a mobile version?**
Not at this time. The desktop experience is the focus.

**How often are updates released?**
Updates follow major game patches and community feedback cycles.
